The Mathematical Superiority of Step-Up SIP

Standard SIP calculations assume you invest the exact same monthly contribution throughout a 15-20 year career. However, real-world salaries typically expand by 8-12% each year. By toggling a 10% Annual Step-Up, an investor allocating ₹10,000/month accumulates over ₹2.1 Crore across 15 years at 12% CAGR, compared to only ~₹50 Lakhs in a static SIP — quadrupling the terminal corpus with modest incremental contributions.
