Tax Provisions & RBI Guidelines

• Section 24(b): Allows deduction up to ₹2,00,000 per financial year against home loan interest paid for a self-occupied property.
• Section 80C: Allows deduction up to ₹1,50,000 for principal repayment (subject to overall Section 80C limit).
• RBI Floating Rate Norm: As per RBI circulars, banks and HFCs cannot levy prepayment foreclosure penalties on floating-rate individual home loans.

Frequently Asked Questions: ₹1 Crore Home Loan EMI Calculator

What is the monthly EMI for a 1 Crore home loan for 20 years?

At 8.5% interest, the EMI for ₹1 Crore for 20 years is ₹86,782/month. Total interest payable is ₹1.08 Crore.

The maths behind the home loan emi calculator

So you can check the result rather than take it on trust.

The formula

EMI = P × r × (1 + r)ⁿ ÷ ((1 + r)ⁿ − 1)

  • ·P — Home loan principal (Sanctioned amount − down payment, typically 80% to 90% LTV)
  • ·r — Monthly interest rate = Annual interest rate ÷ 12 ÷ 100
  • ·n — Loan tenure in months (up to 360 months for 30-year mortgages)

Worked example

A ₹75,00,000 home loan at 8.75% for 25 years.

r (monthly rate)
8.75 ÷ 12 ÷ 100 = 0.0072917
n (months)
25 × 12 = 300
(1 + r)³⁰⁰
1.0072917³⁰⁰ = 8.8131
Monthly EMI
7500000 × 0.0072917 × 8.8131 ÷ 7.8131

EMI = ₹61,659 per month. Total repaid ₹1,84,97,643, of which ₹1,09,97,643 is interest.

Over 25 years at 8.75%, the interest paid (₹1.10 Crore) is 1.46 times the amount borrowed. Prepaying even ₹5,000 extra per month cuts your repayment tenure by over 4 years and saves over ₹22 Lakhs in net interest.

Where this calculation usually goes wrong

Ignoring the Repo Linked Lending Rate (RLLR) resets

All floating rate home loans in India are pegged to the RBI Repo Rate. When the RBI raises or cuts the repo rate, your interest rate and tenure adjust automatically.

Missing tax deductions under Section 24(b) and Section 80C

Under the Old Tax Regime, interest up to ₹2 Lakh/year is deductible under Section 24(b), and principal up to ₹1.5 Lakh under Section 80C. Under the New Tax Regime (Section 115BAC), self-occupied home loan deductions are disallowed.
