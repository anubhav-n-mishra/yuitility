Frequently Asked Questions: 30-Year Fixed Mortgage Calculator

What is the monthly payment on a $400,000 30-year fixed mortgage?

At a 6.85% interest rate with 20% down ($80,000 down payment, $320,000 loan balance), the monthly principal and interest payment is approximately $2,097. Adding estimated taxes and insurance brings the total payment to around $2,613/month.

How does 20% down payment avoid PMI in the US?

Under Fannie Mae and Freddie Mac underwriting rules, putting down 20% or more creates an 80% Loan-to-Value (LTV) ratio, legally exempting conventional borrowers from Private Mortgage Insurance (PMI).
