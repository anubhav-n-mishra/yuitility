Frequently Asked Questions: S&P 500 Index Compound Return Calculator

What is the historical average return of the S&P 500?

Over the last 90+ years, the S&P 500 index has generated an annualized nominal compound return of approximately 10.2% with dividends reinvested (roughly 7% adjusted for inflation).

The maths behind the compound interest calculator

So you can check the result rather than take it on trust.

The formula

FV = P × (1 + i)ⁿ + C × [((1 + i)ⁿ − 1) ÷ i]

  • ·P — starting principal
  • ·C — regular contribution per period
  • ·i — rate per period
  • ·n — number of periods

Worked example

₹1,00,000 starting balance plus ₹5,000 every month for 15 years at 10%.

Total deposited
1,00,000 + (5,000 × 180) = ₹10,00,000
i
10% ÷ 12 = 0.008333
n
180

Final value approximately ₹25,17,744 — growth of ₹15,17,744 on ₹10,00,000 deposited.

Over the same 10 years, ₹1,00,000 at 10% simple interest returns ₹2,00,000 while compound returns ₹2,59,374. The ₹59,374 gap is interest earned on interest, and it widens non-linearly — doubling the period far more than doubles the gap.
