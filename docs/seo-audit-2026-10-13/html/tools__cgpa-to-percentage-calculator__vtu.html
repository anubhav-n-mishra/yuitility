Frequently Asked Questions: VTU CGPA to Percentage Calculator

What is the official VTU formula to convert CGPA to percentage?

The official VTU formula is Percentage = (CGPA - 0.75) * 10. For example, if your VTU CGPA is 8.20, your percentage is (8.20 - 0.75) * 10 = 74.50%.

Which VTU schemes use the (CGPA - 0.75) * 10 formula?

This formula applies to VTU 2015, 2017, 2018, and CBCS schemes across B.E, B.Tech, M.Tech, and MCA programs under Visvesvaraya Technological University.

What is First Class with Distinction in VTU?

Under VTU regulations, a CGPA of 7.75 and above (70.0% or higher) earned in the first attempt qualifies for First Class with Distinction.

The formula

Percentage = CGPA × FormulaMultiplier (CBSE: 9.5, VTU/SPPU: (CGPA − 0.75) × 10, Mumbai Univ Engg: 7.1 × CGPA + 12, KTU/GTU: (CGPA − 0.5) × 10)

  • ·CGPA — Cumulative Grade Point Average on 10.0 scale

  • ·VTU (Karnataka): Percentage = (CGPA − 0.75) × 10
  • ·Anna University (Tamil Nadu): Percentage = CGPA × 10
  • ·Mumbai University: Engineering = 7.1 × CGPA + 12; Arts/Commerce = 7.25 × CGPA + 11
  • ·KTU & GTU: Percentage = (CGPA − 0.5) × 10

Using the generic 9.5 factor for VTU or Mumbai University will produce an inaccurate score on job applications or WES evaluations. Always select your specific graduating university rule.

Where this calculation usually goes wrong

Assuming 9.5 applies to all Indian universities

CBSE popularized the 9.5 multiplier based on the statistical distribution of marks from the 2009–2010 batch. Autonomous universities like VTU, Mumbai University, and Anna University established separate official statutory conversion formulas in their examination ordinances.

Using percentage for WES (World Education Services) evaluation

For foreign university admissions (US/Canada), do not convert your CGPA to percentage manually. Submit your official university grading scale transcript directly.
